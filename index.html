源起西安

Our Story

2016年冬，茶话弄的故事始于十三朝古都西安的一间小茶铺。从传统中国茶文化出发，茶话弄以原叶茶、自然原料和新鲜现泡茶汤，为现代生活带来一杯既有传统味道、又适合年轻人的中国茶饮。

ChaHalo began in Xi’an in the winter of 2016. Xi’an is a historic city known as the heart of thirteen Chinese dynasties. From a small tea shop, ChaHalo grew by bringing traditional Chinese tea culture into modern daily life. We use handpicked tea leaves, natural ingredients, and freshly brewed tea to create drinks that feel both traditional and new.

02

伯克利门店

Our Berkeley Store

茶话弄 Berkeley 位于 UC Berkeley 与伯克利市中心附近，为学生、本地居民和来自各地的游客提供中国茶饮。在上课、学习、工作与通勤的忙碌之间，我们希望一杯香甜的茶饮能让你暂时放松，也让一天的心情变得更好。欢迎来到店里喝杯茶、聊聊天，再重新出发。

Located near UC Berkeley and Downtown Berkeley, ChaHalo Berkeley serves students, local residents, and visitors from around the world. Between classes, study sessions, work, and commuting, we hope a sweet cup of tea can give you a quiet break and make your day a little better. Come in, enjoy a drink, talk with friends, and take a moment to recharge.
